# ADV-001 — Current Advocacy & Reputation Baseline

**Observed:** 2026-08-12  
**Lane:** 02 — Advocacy & Reputation  
**Requirements:** ADV-001, PRIV-001  
**Evidence posture:** public/de-identified only; no customer/student PII is stored here.

## Purpose

Record the first evidence-backed view of AK9I's current feedback, review, testimonial, and referral practices without inferring an operating process from desired future behavior.

## Established observations

### 1. A current public testimonial/review surface exists

AK9I's current website navigation includes a `Testimonial` page at:

- https://ak9i.edu/testimonial/

At the time observed, that page:

- displayed an embedded Google Reviews summary;
- displayed a 4.7 rating based on 75 reviews;
- included a public `review us on` Google link;
- surfaced reviews from multiple AK9I contexts, including student/training, pet-training, and field/service experiences;
- displayed individual public reviews with ages ranging from roughly two to six years on the visible page.

**Interpretation:** AK9I has an active public reputation surface and an accessible path for a visitor to leave a Google review. This is not evidence that AK9I has a defined eligibility, timing, request, follow-up, consent, or attribution workflow for review generation.

### 2. A legacy testimonial page remains publicly indexable

A legacy page remains available at:

- https://ak9i.edu/testimonial-old/

It contains older student testimonial content and is marked by the site's old-page navigation conventions.

**Interpretation:** AK9I has historical student proof, but the continued public availability of both current and legacy testimonial surfaces creates a content-governance question: which proof is current, approved, consent-valid, and intended for reuse?

### 3. Enrollment captures one referral-source signal

The current enrollment form includes a `How you heard about AK9I` section with `Recommendation from a friend or colleague` as an option and requests the referring person's name in the operational form.

Source:

- https://ak9i.edu/enroll

**Interpretation:** AK9I has at least one inbound mechanism that can identify a friend/colleague recommendation at enrollment. The repository must not store the referring person's name. This source-capture mechanism does not by itself establish an outbound referral-ask process, referral eligibility, source persistence, or referral outcome measurement.

### 4. No public evidence of a defined advocacy funnel was found in the inspected site surface

The inspected current site/search surface did not establish a public workflow for:

- advocate eligibility;
- post-completion feedback timing;
- NPS or another approved satisfaction measure;
- testimonial request and consent state;
- referral ask and follow-up;
- advocacy request status;
- referral outcome linkage;
- repeat-advocate management;
- explicit proof approval for downstream marketing reuse.

**Important:** absence from the inspected public site is not proof that these practices do not exist internally. Internal process/system evidence is still required.

## Immediate gaps / unknowns

1. Human accountable owner for advocacy/reputation operations.
2. Authoritative system(s) holding feedback, review request, testimonial, consent, and referral activity.
3. Whether AK9I currently sends surveys or review requests and at what journey moments.
4. Whether an approved satisfaction measure/NPS is currently in use.
5. Current testimonial permission/consent provenance and allowed-use scope.
6. Rules distinguishing student/graduate advocacy from pet-training/service-customer reputation.
7. Criteria supplied by Lane 03 for a graduate/student to become advocacy-eligible.
8. Whether negative/neutral feedback is routed into service recovery before an advocacy ask.
9. Whether referral source captured at enrollment is preserved as original source under the shared attribution contract.
10. Whether a referral can be tied to a resulting qualified prospect, enrollment, completion, or outcome without manual reconstruction.

## Required next evidence

Obtain de-identified/aggregate evidence from authorized AK9I humans and authoritative systems for:

- current feedback/survey instruments and send triggers;
- review-request practice and channel;
- testimonial collection and consent/approval records;
- referral-ask scripts/process and source capture;
- aggregate counts sufficient to distinguish eligible → asked → completed → resulting referral/outcome where available;
- current owners and handoffs;
- any complaint/service-recovery path that should precede advocacy eligibility.

## Cross-lane dependencies surfaced

- **Lane 03 Outcomes:** advocacy eligibility and timing must not assume an outcome is verified merely because a person completed training.
- **Lane 01 Shared Platform:** physical source/consent/referral fields and original-source persistence require system truth before authoritative reporting.
- **Lane 06 Marketing:** public proof may be consumed only after consent/provenance and claim scope are approved; this lane does not authorize marketing publication.
- **Lane 09 Integration:** eventual test must cover 03 → 02 → 06/01 and referral return to qualified demand.

## Baseline disposition

`ADV-001` discovery is **ACTIVE, NOT COMPLETE**.

Evidence establishes that AK9I already has public review/testimonial assets and at least one inbound friend/colleague source-capture point. It does **not** yet establish a repeatable advocacy operating process or measurable closed-loop advocacy funnel.
