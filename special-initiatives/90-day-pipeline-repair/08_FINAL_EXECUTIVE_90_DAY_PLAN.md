# 1. Executive decision

AK9I should be managed for the next 90 days as a **turnaround with a customer-pipeline problem**, not as a marketing campaign with an operations problem.

The company appears to retain meaningful assets: a long-standing working-dog brand, vocational training and service capability, existing students and graduates, employer and veteran-market relevance, Salesforce/trade-show history, canine sales and service opportunities, physical facilities, public reputation assets, and strategic concepts that may create future recurring revenue. However, leadership-reported concerns around cash/payroll, withdrawals, spending, authority, access continuity, culture, records, and execution require verification and containment before those assets can be safely amplified.

The governing posture is **Scenario 3 — Controlled Recovery + Gated Growth**:

1. protect payroll, students, canines, safety, compliance, cash, and institutional access;
2. replace anecdote with an authoritative operating baseline;
3. install decision rights, management cadence, role ownership, and minimum controls;
4. repair completion, verified outcomes, employers, alumni, advocacy, learning, and admissions;
5. activate only the demand and revenue channels the repaired operation can receive and measure;
6. preserve large ideas in a stage-gated portfolio that cannot displace the turnaround.

## Executive approvals required at launch

Leadership should approve or modify:

- the temporary decision-rights matrix;
- the 13-week cash and payroll-control process;
- the spending/procurement exception process;
- the evidence-preservation and confidential escalation protocol;
- current-student, canine-welfare, safety, and delivery-capacity gates;
- critical-account access and cybersecurity controls;
- the Day 14, 30, 60, and 90 gate authority;
- the strategic-bets capacity limit;
- the real resource-loaded calendar for the engagement.

Creation of this plan does not itself authorize personnel termination, compensation or profit-share commitments, vendor or capital commitments, regulated-program changes, legal action, public placement claims, accreditation representations, or new customer-facing claims.

# 2. Evidence and decision boundaries

## Evidence classes

Every material statement and decision should be classified as:

- **FACT** — established by an authoritative record or adopted authority;
- **OBSERVATION** — directly observed, with date/context;
- **LEADERSHIP/EMPLOYEE REPORT** — evidence that the person reported or believes something, not proof of the underlying event;
- **INFERENCE** — reasoned conclusion requiring validation;
- **PROPOSAL** — recommended future action or target;
- **UNKNOWN — BASELINE REQUIRED** — not yet supportable.

## Sensitive matters

- Customer/student PII remains in authorized operational systems, not GitHub.
- Confidential personnel, legal, wage, investigation, medical, criminal-history, and misconduct details remain in the appropriate protected process.
- Credible concerns are preserved and routed to qualified legal, accounting, HR, accreditation, safety, or cybersecurity professionals as appropriate.
- The turnaround team installs controls and establishes facts; it does not publicly accuse or conduct an improvised forensic investigation.
- A written no-retaliation instruction and confidential reporting route should cover wage, safety, animal-welfare, student-record, financial, contract, and compliance concerns.

# 3. Day-90 success condition

By Day 90, AK9I leadership should be able to answer—without reconstructing the business from memory—seven questions:

1. **Cash and payroll:** What cash is available, committed, expected, and at risk over the next 13 weeks, and will scheduled payroll be funded and accurate?
2. **Safety and continuity:** Are students, employees, canines, facilities, records, and required services safe and sufficiently supported to continue instruction?
3. **People and authority:** Who owns each critical outcome, what may they decide, what evidence demonstrates performance, and who provides backup?
4. **Pipeline:** Where is every meaningful active prospect, applicant, student, graduate, employer, advocate, and referral in the governed journey; what happens next; and who owns it?
5. **Outcomes:** What completion and post-graduation results are verified, what remains unknown, and what follow-up or evidence is due?
6. **Growth:** Which channels, partners, referrals, contracts, and campaigns create qualified demand, successful starts, completions, outcomes, and cash contribution?
7. **Management system:** Which controls and routines are operating, which exceptions remain, and what is the next verified enterprise bottleneck?

## Required Day-90 handoff standard

Every critical recurring process should have:

`purpose → accountable owner → backup → procedure → system/source → KPI → cadence → evidence → exception path → review date`

Day 90 is not “transformation complete.” It is the handoff of a controlled, measurable management system and an evidence-based 180-day roadmap.

# 4. Non-negotiable operating rules

1. Protect current students before increasing volume.
2. Payroll, safety, animal welfare, student continuity, and regulatory integrity are veto conditions—not merely dashboard metrics.
3. No material purchase or commitment without visibility and appropriate authorization.
4. No critical process or organizational account should depend on one person’s credentials or undocumented knowledge.
5. No employee decision based solely on rumor, faction, personality, or loyalty narratives; use documented expectations, evidence, consistent process, and professional HR/legal review where needed.
6. No constructive-discharge tactics, retaliation, public accusation, or unapproved incentive promise.
7. No public course, approval, tuition, career, placement, or outcome claim without canonical facts and appropriate approval.
8. No placement/outcome counted without the required evidence.
9. No marketing channel declared successful merely because it creates attention or raw leads.
10. No start cohort or growth campaign without visible instructor, canine, facility, housing, equipment, student-service, and admissions capacity.
11. A control is complete only when it has an owner, procedure, record, evidence, exception path, and test result.
12. Every priority has an owner, next action, due date, evidence, and escalation trigger.
13. No strategic idea outranks payroll, students, canines, compliance, safety, cash, or the current bottleneck.

# 6. Management authority and RACI

The exact names require executive confirmation. The operating model should distinguish:

| Role | Primary accountability | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| CEO / Executive Sponsor | Direction, material decisions, cash/risk priorities, personnel and external commitments | Sole owner of every routine purchase or task |
| Transformation Lead | Integrate workstreams, establish evidence/cadence/controls, challenge assumptions, surface decisions | Permanent owner of every function or a yes-person |
| Finance/Payroll Owner | Cash, payroll, AR/AP, reconciliations, spend records, forecast | Sole initiator/approver/payer/reconciler |
| School/Compliance Owner | Program facts, student records, attendance/completion, accreditation calendar | Informal memory-based authority without evidence |
| Training/Kennel Leads | Qualified instruction, canine welfare, capacity, safety, records | Unreviewed autonomous standards or undocumented changes |
| Admissions/CRM Owner | Inquiry-to-start queue, ownership, stages, source, next action | Private inbox or orphaned records |
| Outcomes/Placement Owner | Completion handoff, assistance, evidence, employer/alumni follow-up | Placement claims without verification |
| Marketing/Partnership Owners | Approved claims, channel execution, attribution, relationship next action | Unapproved spend/content or logo-count reporting |
| HR/Legal/Accounting/Security Advisors | Professional review in their competence | Routine operating ownership unless explicitly engaged |

## Segregation-of-duties rule

For material transactions, separate where practicable:

`request/initiate → approve → receive/verify → enter/pay → reconcile/review`

Where staffing prevents full separation, document compensating independent review.

# Phase 2 — Days 15–30: Install the operating spine

## Objective

Convert the baseline into repeatable management routines and minimum viable controls.

## Required actions

### Management cadence

- daily 15-minute stabilization huddle while needed;
- weekly operating review;
- weekly cash/payroll/AR/AP review;
- weekly student/canine/safety/capacity review;
- weekly admissions/pipeline review;
- biweekly people/capability review;
- monthly strategic-bets review separate from core operations.

Meeting discipline:

`metric/evidence → variance/exception → root cause → decision → owner → next action → due date`

### Role and accountability

- finalize key role scorecards and decision rights;
- assign process owners and backups;
- stop, automate, reassign, or resource orphaned work;
- begin documented coaching/performance processes where evidence supports them;
- do not attach compensation or discipline to unvalidated KPIs.

### Financial controls

- operate weekly forecast reconciliation;
- establish vendor master and payment controls;
- implement reconciliations and independent review;
- test a transaction sample;
- disposition and age exceptions;
- establish delegated permanent approval tiers after emergency centralization.

### Salesforce / pipeline MVP

Implement or validate the minimum operating view:

- canonical identity/reference;
- lifecycle stage and evidence;
- original/referral/partner source where supportable;
- owner;
- next action and due date;
- last meaningful contact;
- program interest and funding route;
- application/enrollment/start status;
- reason closed/lost/hold/withdrawn where applicable;
- data-confidence and source date.

Use governed references to education systems rather than duplicating regulated records inappropriately.

### Admissions standard work

`Inquiry → qualification → program/funding fit → application → documents → interview → enrollment-ready → enrolled → confirmed start → started`

Define entrance, evidence, owner, service expectation, next action, exit, and hold/loss reason for each stage. Create daily queue and stale-record report.

### Student/canine/training operating model

- define student status and completion authority;
- establish attendance/withdrawal triggers and interventions;
- create first-week, midpoint, and pre-completion touchpoints where appropriate;
- activate complaint/service recovery;
- establish canine welfare, training record, incident, assessment, remediation, and capacity routines;
- define continuity escalation triggers.

### Outcome/placement MVP

`Completion → eligible graduate → goal → assistance → activity → interview/offer/start or other governed outcome → verification → follow-up/close → alumni handoff`

Define acceptable verification, written policy, owner, rolling management view, and evidence freshness. Do not publish performance claims until cohort, numerator, denominator, exclusions, time window, and supporting records are defensible.

### Marketing/publication QA

`request → canonical facts → draft → fact/compliance/claim review → brand/quality review → authorization → publication → version/archive → performance review`

- operating cadence has run and actions are closing;
- payroll/calendar/control review complete and scheduled payroll risk visible;
- forecast reconciled and variance explained;
- sampled spend controls tested and exceptions owned;

# Phase 3 — Days 31–45: Repair completion, outcomes, employers, alumni, and advocacy

## Objective

Make the back half of the pipeline an operating engine and feedback source.

## Actions

### Outcome recovery

- build authorized/de-identified recent graduate cohorts needing follow-up;
- segment verified employment, continuing employment, self-employment needing verification, seeking, not seeking where allowed, unreachable, and unknown;
- conduct structured follow-up;
- capture assistance and evidence state;
- treat missing data as backlog, not inferred success/failure.

### Employers

- classify relationship as signal/contact, active relationship, active hiring relationship, or measurable contribution;
- assign owner and next action;
- capture current hiring needs, candidate requirements, location/pay where appropriate, and willingness to engage;
- match graduates through authorized systems;
- feed readiness feedback into curriculum and services.

### Alumni and advocacy

- inventory contact permissions/channels;
- re-engage with value: career updates, continuing education, employer introductions, resources, events;
- request structured outcome updates;
- define advocacy eligibility as successful experience/outcome plus consent—not mere completion;
- collect feedback before requesting review/testimonial/referral;
- route neutral/negative feedback to service recovery;
- preserve provenance and link referrals to pipeline state/outcome.

## Day-45 evidence

- categorized outcome backlog and trend;
- employer roster with owners/current needs;
- alumni reactivation process;
- feedback/service-recovery/review/referral workflow;
- first de-identified closed-loop trace examples;
- curriculum/service feedback register.

# 12. Core control requirements

## Purchasing and payment

`need → request → purpose → budget/cash check → quote/reasonableness → approval → purchase → receipt/service confirmation → invoice/coding/payment → reconciliation → exception review`

Required additions:

- vendor-master change control;
- related-party/conflict review;
- three-way match where applicable;
- duplicate/unusual payment review;
- independent bank/payment reconciliation;
- delegated approval tiers after emergency centralization;
- sample testing and exception aging.

## Payroll/time

- one time/PTO system;
- accurate hours and approvals;
- payroll calendar and cutoffs;
- register/deduction/funding/tax review;
- written handling of overtime/comp time consistent with law/policy;
- payroll schedule changes only after provider/counsel review and written transition plan.

## Digital continuity and cybersecurity

For each critical system record business owner, primary/backup admin, MFA, organization recovery, billing, least privilege, backup/export, last access review, offboarding, and incident owner. Secrets never enter GitHub.

## Marketing/publication

One canonical current fact source; request/draft/fact/claim/brand/approval/publication/version/performance evidence. Testimonials and outcomes require provenance and permission.

## Contracts/government

Central file for solicitation/amendments, registrations/certifications, conflicts, partners, cost/pricing, capacity, cash/margin, authorized submitter, version, award, deliverables, modifications, invoicing, acceptance, renewal/termination, and closeout.

## People

Role and evidence-based expectations; consistent coaching/discipline; confidential issue handling; no retaliation; no public allegations; no constructive-discharge tactics; no unapproved compensation promises.

## Canine/safety/training quality

Daily welfare/health/custody controls; incident/near-miss route; emergency/veterinary escalation; controlled training/assessment/remediation records; applicable standards and program/customer requirements; periodic quality review.

## AI/LMS/data

Purpose limitation, data map, legal/privacy/security review, notice/consent where applicable, retention/access, accuracy test, human approval, correction, incident path, and no unreviewed adverse decisions.

# 16. External standards used as reviewer benchmarks

These references inform the reviewer additions; they do not convert unverified AK9I conditions into facts.

- **ACCET Maintaining Accreditation:** completion/placement records should be orderly, accurate, comprehensive, and verifiable; vocational data instruments are maintained at least every 90 days and used as management tools; financial resources must support quality training and completion of instruction for enrolled participants.
- **Virginia Code § 40.1-29:** establishes regular pay-period, wage-payment, pay-statement, and recordkeeping requirements; specific application should be reviewed with the payroll provider and qualified counsel.
- **U.S. GAO Green Book (2025, effective FY2026):** useful control framework for operations, reliable reporting, compliance, fraud/improper-payment, and information-security risk; AK9I may use it proportionally as a nonfederal management benchmark.
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 Small Business Quick-Start Guide:** proportional cybersecurity-risk model applicable to small/medium organizations and schools.
- **NIST OSAC / ANSI/ASB Standard 088-20:** relevant benchmark for training, certification, and documentation in canine detection disciplines where applicable.
- Existing AK9I governing repository authority and evidence register remain the primary program context.
