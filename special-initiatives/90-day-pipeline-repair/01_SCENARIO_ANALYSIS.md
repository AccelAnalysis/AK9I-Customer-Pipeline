# Scenario Analysis — Repairing and Bolstering the AK9I Pipeline

## Executive framing

AK9I should be managed for the next 90 days as a **turnaround with a pipeline problem**, not as a marketing campaign with an operations problem.

Current durable repository truth still says the actual operating baseline is incomplete. Executive-reported observations add urgency: payroll has reportedly been late, student starts/attendance reportedly declined sharply, withdrawals were reportedly very high during a recent period, operating relationships and expenses have not always been governed consistently, and key digital/company accounts may have depended too heavily on individual administrators. These statements are management signals requiring verification; they are not treated here as audited facts.

At the same time, AK9I retains meaningful assets:

- a recognized working-dog brand operating since 2001;
- current training, service, pet-service, and government/law-enforcement relevance;
- veteran education positioning and current public GI Bill/SkillBridge claims that warrant authoritative verification;
- public employer/recruiter and job-placement support surfaces;
- an existing Salesforce history and trade-show lead-generation behavior reported by leadership;
- an alumni base measured in the thousands according to executive statements;
- public review/testimonial assets;
- an existing partner ecosystem and contractor/government opportunity surface;
- canine sales/service revenue capability;
- facilities, kennels, dormitory capacity, and other physical assets;
- potential adjacent products such as continuing education, LMS content, events, media, and technology.

The recovery strategy therefore should protect the core, establish truth and controls, then selectively reactivate the assets that can generate near-term cash and long-term pipeline health.

---

## Scenario 0 — Continue Current Operating Pattern

### Description

Continue pursuing contracts, sales, events, marketing, and new ideas while repairing issues opportunistically as they surface.

### Advantages

- minimal disruption;
- avoids difficult near-term decisions;
- can produce episodic revenue wins.

### Failure modes

- cash continues to leak through weak approval, purchasing, timekeeping, or vendor practices;
- payroll instability undermines employee trust and delivery quality;
- high lead volume is wasted if admissions/student support/outcomes remain unstable;
- public messaging remains inconsistent;
- institutional knowledge and digital access remain person-dependent;
- management attention fragments across urgent problems and attractive new ideas;
- strategic projects can become substitutes for fixing the operating core.

### Assessment

**Not recommended.** This is the path most likely to create activity without durable recovery.

---

## Scenario 1 — Survival Containment

### Description

For 30–45 days, place the company in strict stabilization mode: freeze discretionary spending, centralize approvals, focus on current students, payroll, cash collection, critical sales/contracts, and compliance.

### Primary actions

- 13-week cash forecast and weekly cash-control meeting;
- pre-approval for discretionary spend and reimbursements;
- time/PTO/expense enforcement;
- contract, receivable, payable, and vendor review;
- critical account access redundancy;
- current-student attendance/withdrawal rescue;
- staffing-capacity protection;
- suspend nonessential initiatives.

### Advantages

- fastest reduction in uncontrolled risk;
- protects payroll and instruction;
- establishes management authority;
- creates breathing room for evidence collection.

### Risks

- can become overly defensive;
- can damage morale if controls are framed as punishment;
- may underinvest in revenue recovery;
- does not by itself rebuild the closed-loop pipeline.

### Best use

Use as the **first 10–14 days** of the recommended strategy, not as the full 90-day strategy.

---

## Scenario 2 — Pipeline Reconstruction First

### Description

Concentrate the full 90 days on Salesforce, lifecycle definitions, admissions workflow, learning/student success, completion/outcome tracking, alumni/referrals, employer relationships, marketing attribution, and KPI reporting.

### Advantages

- directly aligns with the AK9I Customer Pipeline program;
- produces durable operating visibility;
- supports accreditation/compliance evidence;
- reduces dependence on memory and individuals;
- creates the foundation for scalable marketing.

### Risks

- process redesign can fail if immediate cash/control issues are not stabilized first;
- can become a systems project while culture, authority, and execution remain weak;
- CRM configuration alone cannot repair service quality or accountability.

### Best use

Make this the **core work from Day 8 through Day 60**, after containment has begun.

---

## Scenario 3 — Controlled Recovery + Gated Growth **(Recommended)**

### Description

Combine survival containment, pipeline reconstruction, and selective revenue/growth activation through explicit gates.

### Operating principle

**Stabilize → instrument → repair from the end backward → prove capacity → scale.**

### Four concurrent tracks

#### Track A — Enterprise stabilization

Cash, payroll reliability, spend controls, account continuity, contracts, asset controls, authority, management cadence, role clarity, HR/timekeeping, and escalation.

#### Track B — Closed-loop pipeline repair

Outcomes/placement → alumni/advocacy → learning/student success → admissions → demand, supported horizontally by Salesforce/shared data and partnerships.

#### Track C — Revenue preservation/reactivation

Protect current canine sales, service contracts, existing students, credible government opportunities, employer relationships, and warm historic leads. Revenue work proceeds only with ownership, margin visibility, and execution capacity.

#### Track D — Strategic bets sandbox

LMS, closed-loop AI/kennel assistant, Working Line K9, CLDA/TSA training, events/media, and real-estate/asset monetization are evaluated in parallel but cannot consume turnaround-critical capacity without passing a gate.

### Why this is recommended

It recognizes that AK9I appears to have both a **survival problem and an under-monetized-asset problem**. Focusing only on austerity leaves growth on the table; focusing only on growth recreates the underlying failure conditions. The solution is controlled simultaneity with hard decision gates.

---

## Scenario 4 — Aggressive Growth / Platform Expansion

### Description

Immediately launch high-volume Salesforce/ZoomInfo outreach, LMS subscriptions, Working Line K9, CLDA/TSA training, regional events, new partnerships, new courses, and other large ideas while rebuilding the core in parallel.

### Advantages

- potentially large upside;
- can energize the organization;
- may create non-tuition revenue diversification.

### Risks

- highest execution risk;
- regulated-program and advertising claims may outrun approvals;
- high-volume demand can overwhelm admissions/instruction;
- distracts leadership from cash, culture, controls, and student outcomes;
- large market-size/revenue assumptions in executive conversations remain unverified;
- could increase liability if technology, privacy, accreditation, or TSA/other regulatory requirements are not validated first.

### Assessment

**Do not use as the 90-day core strategy.** Preserve these ideas in the strategic-bets backlog and advance only narrow discovery/pilots.

# Readiness gates for moving from repair to growth

Growth should expand only when the following evidence exists. These are planning gates, not claims that AK9I currently satisfies them.

## Gate 1 — Financial/operating control

- payroll obligations have a visible funding plan;
- a 13-week cash forecast is in active weekly use;
- discretionary spend and reimbursements follow an approved workflow;
- material contracts/opportunities have an owner, due date, margin/cash-impact view, and approval status;
- critical company accounts have continuity access;
- no material operational function depends on one person's credentials or undocumented knowledge.

## Gate 2 — Current-student protection

- every active student has a current status, attendance position, completion risk, owner, and next action;
- instructor capacity is sufficient for the enrolled/start pipeline;
- withdrawal reasons are coded and reviewed;
- student complaints/service issues have a triage path;
- course requirements and customer-facing information are canonical and consistent.

## Gate 3 — Pipeline observability

- Salesforce/authoritative systems have a defined owner;
- active inquiry/applicant/student records can be assigned to governed stages;
- every meaningful active record has owner + next action + due date;
- original/referral source is preserved where available;
- admissions and marketing can reconcile lead → applicant → enrollment-ready → start without manual reconstruction.

## Gate 4 — Outcome integrity

- completion is defined from actual operating evidence;
- eligible graduates enter a documented placement/outcome process;
- employer contact, interview, offer, acceptance, start, self-employment, continuing employment, and verified outcome are not conflated;
- evidence exists to support any placement/outcome claim;
- the process is compatible with current ACCET completion/placement requirements.

## Gate 5 — Delivery capacity

- staffing/instructor coverage can absorb the planned starts;
- facilities/dogs/equipment/housing constraints are visible;
- new demand does not create a foreseeable service failure;
- there is a response plan for foreseeable instructor turnover.

## Gate 6 — Marketing integrity

- current course names, duration, tuition, approval status, and financing information are reconciled;
- marketing materials have owner + QA approval before release;
- testimonials/outcome claims have provenance/permission;
- campaign measurement can distinguish attention from qualified pipeline contribution.

When Gates 1–6 are substantially met, AK9I can move from controlled recovery into repeatable growth.

# Key scenario conclusions

1. **The first bottleneck is management control, not lead generation.** A pipeline cannot be healthy if cash, authority, time, access, spending, and execution are not visible.
2. **The second bottleneck is current-student success.** Increasing enrollment while withdrawals/delivery risk are unresolved destroys cash, reputation, outcomes, and referrals.
3. **The third bottleneck is verified outcomes.** ACCET's current guidance makes completion/placement data an operating management tool, not merely an annual reporting exercise.
4. **The fourth bottleneck is lifecycle/source truth.** Salesforce should become the operating spine only after the actual systems, fields, ownership, and handoffs are understood.
5. **AK9I's fastest durable growth assets are probably already in hand:** current students, historical leads, alumni, employers/partners, reviews, existing canine/service relationships, and veteran/law-enforcement market credibility.
6. **LMS/AI/Working Line K9/CLDA are potentially meaningful multipliers, but they are not substitutes for repairing AK9I.** They should be treated as gated ventures with separate business cases.
7. **The 90-day goal is not “finish the transformation.”** It is to leave AK9I with a controlled operating system, a measurable pipeline, repaired downstream loops, credible management routines, and evidence that shows what should scale next.
