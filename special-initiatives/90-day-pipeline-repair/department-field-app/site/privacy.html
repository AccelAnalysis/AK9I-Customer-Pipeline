Privacy and data handling

This fieldwork application is designed for operational discovery. Responses are associated with the self-identified work email entered by the respondent and are not anonymous.

Do not enter

  • student or customer names or other personally identifiable records;
  • passwords, access codes, recovery keys, or payment credentials;
  • medical information or confidential personnel information;
  • allegations about named individuals.

For sensitive concerns, select Private follow-up requested. The general form intentionally does not collect the sensitive explanation.

Where responses go

The GitHub Pages site contains only application code. Submitted responses are sent to a protected Firebase HTTPS endpoint and stored in a private Cloud Firestore collection. Public read access is disabled. No response data is written to GitHub.

Local draft

Until submission succeeds, this device stores a local draft so a browser refresh or brief connectivity interruption does not erase progress. The draft is removed after successful submission. The work email can remain remembered on the device for convenience.
