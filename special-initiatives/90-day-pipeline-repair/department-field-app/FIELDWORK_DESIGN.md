# Tuesday Department Fieldwork Design

**Date:** August 18, 2026  
**Purpose:** turn individual operating signals and department discussion into a comparable, evidence-oriented operating map without turning the meeting into a long-form employee investigation.

## Two connected forms

### Individual Department Operating Pulse

Completed by each participant on their phone. It gathers comparable signals about:

- role and department context;
- mission, role, authority, workload, handoffs, capacity, systems, safety/quality, backup coverage, and 30-day delivery confidence;
- normal work, out-of-role work, work falling through cracks, wait states, common delay causes, and systems used;
- current versus desired measurement;
- waste and off-process spending/time patterns at category level;
- single-person functional dependencies;
- immediate operating-risk categories and urgency without sensitive detail;
- department-specific operating constraints;
- three enterprise priorities, a seven-day improvement, evidence sources, and willingness to help.

### Department Meeting Summary

Completed once by the facilitator after the meeting. It records agreed operating truth at the role/process level:

- purpose;
- five recurring work activities;
- accountable role, overlapping responsibility, and orphaned work;
- systems and authoritative source, if known;
- up to three handoffs and failure modes;
- current measurement and three candidate KPIs with owner/source;
- three material failure points;
- GREEN/YELLOW/RED/UNKNOWN risk status across nine categories;
- immediate fixes with role owner, date, priority, and completion evidence;
- evidence requests;
- unresolved questions and private escalation category.

## Evidence discipline

Individual responses are perceptions and operating signals. They do not become verified facts simply because multiple people selected the same answer. The fieldwork team should compare material claims with systems, records, direct observation, and accountable-role confirmation.

The application intentionally asks for functions, roles, categories, and evidence locations instead of allegations about named individuals. Confidential HR, legal, safety, student, financial, or investigative details remain in the appropriate private process.

## Meeting workflow

1. **0–5 minutes:** explain purpose and confidentiality boundaries.
2. **5–12 minutes:** participants complete the Pulse from the department QR code.
3. **12–30 minutes:** map current work, systems, and handoffs.
4. **30–45 minutes:** discuss repeated failure points and lowest-rated areas.
5. **45–55 minutes:** agree one or two immediate fixes and evidence requests.
6. **After the meeting:** facilitator completes the Department Meeting Summary.

## Required output

The meeting produces the Day-2 operating map:

`Purpose → Work → Owner → System → Dependency → KPI → Failure Point → Immediate Fix`
