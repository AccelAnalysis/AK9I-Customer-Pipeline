# D. Accreditation / education-sector evidence

## ACCET — Maintaining Accreditation

https://accet.org/accreditation/maintaining-accreditation

Current ACCET guidance states, in substance:

- completion/placement records must be orderly, comprehensive, accurate, and verifiable;
- vocational data collection instruments are maintained at least every 90 days and should function as management tools;
- accredited institutions must demonstrate responsible financial management and resources sufficient to maintain quality training and complete instruction for enrolled participants.

**Planning implication:** AK9I's outcome process and financial stabilization are not separate from education quality. Completion/placement and financial capacity belong on the operating scorecard.

## ACCET — Documents & Forms

https://accet.org/documents-forms

# G. Key external-evidence conclusions used in the plan

1. **Outcome verification is a management function.** Current ACCET guidance supports treating completion/placement records as an active operating management system, not a year-end paperwork task.
2. **Financial stability and instructional continuity are linked.** Cash/payroll control belongs in a customer-pipeline turnaround because enrolled students depend on sustained delivery capacity.
3. **Veteran-channel claims require current program truth.** Virginia SAA is the relevant approval pathway and should be verified at the program level before amplification.
4. **There is broader occupational-market relevance, but no external source substitutes for AK9I outcome evidence.** Labor-market projections can inform positioning and employer strategy; they cannot establish placement performance.
5. **AK9I already has multiple pipeline assets.** Website forms, employer intake, testimonials, tours, courses, services, and a historical operating brand create surfaces to connect; the main unknown is how consistently those surfaces are instrumented, owned, and converted today.
