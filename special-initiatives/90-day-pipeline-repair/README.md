# AK9I 90-Day Pipeline Repair & Bolstering Initiative

**Status:** Strategic planning / executive-directed analysis  
**Created:** 2026-08-16  
**Planning horizon:** First 90 days of the AK9I transformation engagement  
**Program relationship:** Supplements the governed Customer Pipeline program; does not silently replace canonical lifecycle, requirement, acceptance, integration, privacy, or evidence rules.

## Purpose

This folder turns current AK9I evidence, executive direction, pipeline governance, and external industry/regulatory context into an executable 90-day recovery plan.

The operating objective is not simply to increase enrollment. The objective is to stabilize the enterprise sufficiently to operate a measurable closed-loop pipeline:

`qualified demand → enrollment → student success → completion → verified outcome → alumni/advocacy/referral → better qualified demand`

The initiative also recognizes that pipeline performance cannot be repaired while cash leakage, unclear authority, uncontrolled spending, weak account continuity, inconsistent public information, poor role accountability, or unstable delivery capacity remain unresolved.

## Source hierarchy and evidence discipline

This workspace distinguishes four evidence classes:

1. **Governing repository authority** — current merged AK9I program governance and accepted durable state.
2. **Observed/public evidence** — AK9I website, regulator/accreditor guidance, official government/industry sources.
3. **Executive-reported operating observations** — statements supplied by AK9I leadership and engagement conversations. These are important management inputs but must not be converted into accusations, verified metrics, or legal conclusions without appropriate evidence.
4. **Proposals / scenarios** — recommended actions, targets, controls, and decision gates that require executive authorization before being treated as adopted operating policy.

No customer/student PII belongs in this folder. Personnel allegations, protected HR details, legal theories, or unverified misconduct claims should remain outside this public repository and be handled through authorized HR, legal, accounting, or investigative channels.

## Core planning doctrine

The repository's existing architecture is preserved: **repair the pipeline from the end toward the beginning while establishing shared system truth and allowing safe parallel discovery.** That means:

1. stop operational and financial leakage;
2. establish command, ownership, and minimum controls;
3. establish actual pipeline/system truth;
4. stabilize current students and delivery capacity;
5. make completion/outcomes verifiable;
6. reconnect alumni, employers, and advocates;
7. repair admissions and attribution;
8. scale demand only when downstream gates are met;
9. keep larger strategic bets (LMS, AI, Working Line K9, CLDA, real-estate monetization) in governed test lanes so they do not distract from the turnaround.

## Recommended scenario

The recommended posture is **Scenario 3 — Controlled Recovery + Gated Growth**:

- Days 1–14: containment, authority, controls, cash visibility, current-student protection, and evidence collection.
- Days 15–30: operating spine, pipeline truth, role/KPI ownership, student-success controls, outcome verification, and content correction.
- Days 31–60: rebuild the back half of the pipeline (completion → outcome → alumni → advocacy/employer loop) while fixing admissions and attribution.
- Days 61–90: scale only the channels and strategic pilots that pass explicit readiness gates.

## What this initiative does not authorize

Creation of these planning artifacts does **not** by itself authorize personnel termination, compensation changes, profit-share grants, vendor commitments, capital purchases, new regulated training programs, legal action, public placement claims, changes to accreditation representations, or new customer-facing marketing claims. Those remain executive/human decisions subject to the appropriate professional review.
