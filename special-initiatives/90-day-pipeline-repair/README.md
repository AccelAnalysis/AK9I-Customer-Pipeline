## Purpose

This initiative turns current AK9I evidence, executive direction, pipeline governance, and external industry/regulatory context into an executable 90-day recovery plan.

The objective is not simply to increase enrollment. It is to stabilize AK9I sufficiently to operate a measurable closed-loop pipeline:

`qualified demand → enrollment → student success → completion → verified outcome → alumni/advocacy/referral → better qualified demand`

The reviewed edition also makes payroll, student continuity, canine welfare, safety, regulatory integrity, delivery capacity, and evidence-based controls explicit veto conditions for growth.

## Source hierarchy and evidence discipline

This workspace distinguishes:

1. **Governing repository authority** — current merged AK9I program governance and accepted durable state.
2. **Observed/public evidence** — AK9I website, regulator/accreditor guidance, official government/industry sources.
3. **Executive-reported operating observations** — management inputs that require validation and may not be converted into accusations, audited metrics, or legal conclusions without evidence.
4. **Inference** — reasoned interpretation requiring validation.
5. **Proposals / controls / targets** — recommendations requiring executive adoption.
6. **Unknown — baseline required** — the correct state when authoritative evidence is not yet available.

No customer/student PII belongs in this folder. Personnel allegations, protected HR details, legal theories, wage/investigation material, or unverified misconduct claims remain outside the public repository and must be handled through authorized HR, legal, accounting, accreditation, safety, or investigative channels.

## Core planning doctrine

**Stabilize → instrument → repair from the end backward → prove capacity → scale.**

1. protect payroll, students, canines, safety, compliance, cash, and access;
2. stop operational and financial leakage;
3. establish command, ownership, minimum controls, and confidential escalation;
4. establish actual system/process truth;
5. stabilize current students and delivery capacity;
6. make completion/outcomes verifiable;
7. reconnect employers, alumni, advocates, and referrals;
8. repair learning, admissions, public truth, and attribution;
9. scale demand only after objective gates;
10. keep LMS, AI, Working Line K9, CLDA, events, real estate, and other strategic bets in governed test lanes.
