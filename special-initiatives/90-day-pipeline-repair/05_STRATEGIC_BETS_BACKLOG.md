# Strategic Bets Backlog — Protect the Vision Without Distracting the Turnaround

## Purpose

AK9I leadership has articulated a much larger vision than repairing the current school pipeline. The ideas include continuing education, a Learning Management System (LMS), AI-enabled kennel/training support, a Working Line K9 digital/media ecosystem, association-based security training through CLDA, conferences/competitions, broader industry standards, government opportunities, real-estate monetization, solar/asset strategies, and employee wealth-sharing structures.

The 90-day turnaround should **preserve and evaluate these ideas without allowing them to displace payroll, current students, compliance, cash control, or the current pipeline bottleneck.**

This register deliberately separates leadership concepts from verified opportunities. Revenue, audience-size, licensing, membership, regulatory, profitability, and market-size figures mentioned in leadership discussion are treated as **hypotheses until independently validated**.

---

# Stage-gate model

Every strategic bet moves through:

`IDEA → EVIDENCE → BUSINESS CASE → AUTHORIZED PILOT → PILOT EVIDENCE → SCALE / HOLD / STOP`

A bet cannot skip from `IDEA` to `SCALE` because the upside sounds large.

## Required business-case questions

1. Who is the customer and what problem is solved?
2. What evidence shows the problem and willingness to pay?
3. What approvals/accreditation/regulatory constraints apply?
4. What is AK9I's defensible advantage?
5. What is the minimum viable offer/pilot?
6. What systems/data/privacy/security are required?
7. What people and management capacity are required?
8. What upfront cash and ongoing operating cost are required?
9. What are conservative/base/upside unit economics?
10. What current work would be displaced?
11. What metric proves the pilot works?
12. What failure/stop condition prevents sunk-cost escalation?

# LMS strategy within the 90 days

The LMS concept has two very different jobs and should not be blended prematurely.

## Job A — Improve AK9I's existing student/graduate system

Potential uses:

- structured pre-arrival/pre-course preparation;
- supplemental learning;
- instructor resources;
- professional-development modules;
- post-course refreshers;
- alumni continuing education;
- documented learning activity where compliant and appropriate.

This requires integration with AK9I's actual school records, curriculum governance, and accreditation obligations.

## Job B — Create a new external training business

Potential customers discussed include working-dog professionals and non-canine security personnel. This is a separate product/business model requiring market, licensing/regulatory, content, customer-support, payments, identity, reporting, and data-security decisions.

**Recommendation:** During the 90 days, pilot Job A or a narrow alumni continuing-education use case first. Keep the larger external training venture in business-case discovery unless a specific verified customer/partner opportunity warrants acceleration.

# Closed-loop AI / kennel assistant within the 90 days

The useful question is not “Can AI watch the kennels?” It is “Which repetitive observation or documentation task can AI assist without making unsafe, inaccurate, privacy-invasive, or unreviewed decisions?”

Candidate low-risk POC functions:

- index training-session video by date/time/location;
- help staff retrieve relevant clips;
- draft a training-session summary for human approval;
- flag missing expected documentation rather than judge student performance;
- generate a draft LMS/training record from approved inputs;
- help answer internal procedural questions from approved documents.

Avoid in the first POC:

- automated employee discipline;
- automated student pass/fail decisions;
- unreviewed safety/welfare conclusions;
- facial/emotional inference;
- uncontrolled retention of video/PII;
- treating AI-generated observations as authoritative records without human verification.

# CLDA / TSA concept — required diligence before a pilot

Leadership discussion describes a potential model in which an association/program holder and an LMS could support standardized security training at scale. The asserted membership counts, required-course population, transferability, amendment mechanism, pricing, and revenue opportunity are **not established facts in this planning artifact**.

Before treating it as a business opportunity, verify:

1. CLDA legal identity, governance, member count, and authority to sponsor/represent the relevant program holders.
2. The exact TSA-regulated program(s) and training provisions at issue.
3. Whether and under what authority one program holder/authorized representative can seek the contemplated amendment.
4. Whether training completion can transfer across covered program holders and what records must accompany it.
5. Required curriculum, instructor, testing, identity, record-retention, audit, accessibility, cybersecurity, and reporting requirements.
6. Which party carries regulatory accountability.
7. Actual required learner population and training frequency.
8. Commercial willingness to pay and procurement/payment path.
9. LMS integration/API expectations.
10. Pilot population small enough to verify compliance and support burden before scale.

No market-size or revenue forecast should be used in financing, hiring, or spending decisions before this diligence is complete.

# Portfolio governance

The monthly strategic-bets review should display only:

- stage;
- evidence added since last review;
- next gate;
- cash/people consumed;
- expected decision date;
- decision: `advance / pilot / hold / stop`.

If a strategic bet cannot identify its next falsifiable question, it is not ready to consume turnaround resources.
