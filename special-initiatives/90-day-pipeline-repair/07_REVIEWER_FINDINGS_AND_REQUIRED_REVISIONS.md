# What the source plan gets right

## 1. Correct turnaround thesis

The plan does not attempt to solve a downstream service and outcome problem by immediately buying more leads. It sequences containment, baseline truth, student protection, completion/outcome repair, alumni/employer activation, admissions repair, and gated demand growth.

## 2. Appropriate evidence discipline

The source package consistently distinguishes repository authority, public evidence, executive-reported observations, inference, proposal, and verified operating fact. This is especially important because the leadership source includes candid personnel, spending, contract, culture, and suspected-misconduct concerns that cannot responsibly be converted into public accusations or management conclusions without evidence.

## 3. Strong first-two-week fieldwork

The Day 1–14 work is practical: trace cash, current students, systems, departments, procurement, contracts, the actual customer journey, withdrawals, completion/outcomes, employers, alumni, and role accountability before redesigning the future state.

## 4. Strong closed-loop pipeline architecture

The plan preserves the governed journey and correctly treats completion/outcome evidence, alumni/advocacy, learning quality, admissions, attribution, and demand as one operating system rather than disconnected projects.

## 5. Proper treatment of strategic bets

LMS, closed-loop AI, Working Line K9, CLDA/TSA training, conferences, standards, real estate, solar, and employee-wealth concepts are preserved without allowing unverified market-size or revenue estimates to displace payroll, students, compliance, or the current bottleneck.

# Required revisions

## R1 — Make payroll compliance and payroll-calendar governance explicit

### Finding

The source plan appropriately prioritizes payroll funding, but it does not fully separate three distinct questions:

1. Is every earned wage paid accurately and on the established pay date?
2. Is the current pay schedule legally and operationally compliant?
3. Would changing pay cadence improve cash administration without creating employee harm, payroll errors, or a disguised liquidity workaround?

### Required revision

- Establish a documented payroll calendar for the next 13 weeks.
- Reconcile payroll register, time records, deductions, funding date, tax/deposit obligations, and bank settlement before each payroll.
- Add **scheduled payroll paid accurately and on time** to the executive scorecard.
- Route any proposed change from biweekly to semimonthly/twice-monthly—or any other pay-period change—through the payroll provider and qualified employment counsel before adoption.
- Communicate any approved change in writing with effective date, transition-period treatment, employee impact, and escalation contact.
- Do not treat a pay-frequency change as a substitute for adequate payroll funding.

Virginia law establishes regular-pay-period requirements and specifies minimum pay frequency by employee type; this is a legal-review item, not a management improvisation.

## R2 — Add counsel-directed evidence preservation, protected reporting, and anti-retaliation controls

### Finding

The source plan correctly says to preserve evidence and route credible concerns to professionals. It should more clearly prevent a turnaround team from becoming an informal investigation channel, contaminating evidence, creating discoverability problems, or exposing employees who report concerns.

### Required revision

- CEO/counsel identifies the scope, custodian, hold instructions, access restrictions, and review method for sensitive evidence.
- Establish a confidential reporting path for wage, safety, animal-welfare, financial, retaliation, student-record, contract, or compliance concerns.
- Add a written no-retaliation instruction.
- Preserve need-to-know access and chain-of-custody where appropriate.
- Do not conduct accusatory interviews, public debates, or unilateral forensic review through GitHub.
- Separate corrective operating controls from conclusions about individual misconduct.

## R3 — Strengthen segregation of duties and control testing

### Finding

The source guardrails provide approvals and records, but effective internal control also requires separating incompatible duties and testing whether the control actually operated.

### Required revision

For material transactions, separate where staffing permits:

`request/initiate → approve → receive/verify → enter/pay → reconcile/review`

No one person should routinely initiate, approve, receive, pay, and reconcile the same transaction. Where staffing makes separation impracticable, document a compensating independent review.

Add:

- vendor-master change approval;
- bank and payment-platform reconciliation with independent review;
- three-way match where applicable: purchase authorization, receipt/service confirmation, invoice;
- duplicate-payment and unusual-vendor review;
- related-party/vendor conflict disclosure;
- sample-based control testing at Days 14, 30, 60, and 90;
- exception aging and recurring-exception root cause.

The control standard is not “a policy exists.” It is “the control operated, produced evidence, and exceptions were dispositioned.”

## R4 — Add a dedicated canine safety, welfare, and training-quality operating layer

### Finding

The source fieldwork includes kennel care and training records, but the executive plan and scorecard do not elevate animal welfare, student/employee safety, and working-dog training quality to explicit turnaround gates.

### Required revision

Add a daily/weekly operating layer covering:

- canine identity, assignment, health, feeding, medication, veterinary status, housing, exercise, sanitation, transport, and custody;
- bite, escape, injury, illness, heat/cold exposure, equipment, environmental, and near-miss reporting;
- emergency coverage and veterinary escalation;
- student/employee safety incidents and corrective action;
- current training plan and session records;
- scenario-based proficiency assessment;
- evaluator authority and independence appropriate to the program;
- remediation, reassessment, certification/recertification, and record completeness;
- controlled change to training standards and curricula.

The NIST OSAC registry includes ANSI/ASB Standard 088-20 for training, certification, and documentation of canine detection disciplines. AK9I should use applicable governing standards and customer/program requirements as a benchmark rather than relying only on instructor memory or reputation.

## R5 — Add student-continuity and teach-out escalation triggers

### Finding

The source plan protects active students and freezes impulsive instructor changes, but it does not define when cash, staffing, facility, dog, equipment, or accreditation risk requires a formal continuity or regulator/accreditor discussion.

### Required revision

Create a confidential continuity decision tree with triggers such as:

- inability to fund scheduled instruction or payroll needed to deliver it;
- loss of qualified instructional coverage without replacement;
- unsafe or unavailable facility/dog/equipment capacity;
- material interruption of student records or required services;
- inability to meet approved program requirements;
- risk that enrolled participants cannot complete instruction as represented.

For each trigger, define immediate mitigation, executive owner, counsel/compliance consultation, required notification analysis, student communication authority, and contingency/teach-out planning. This is a preparedness control, not a prediction that closure will occur.

ACCET expressly links financial management to resources sufficient to maintain quality training and complete instruction for enrolled participants.

## R6 — Make cybersecurity, continuity, and AI governance testable

### Finding

The source plan requires backup administrators and flags AI risk, but account continuity alone is not a complete security control.

### Required revision

For critical systems, require:

- organization-controlled accounts and recovery methods;
- MFA where supported;
- least-privilege access;
- password-manager/credential-custody standard outside GitHub;
- backup/export and recovery verification;
- access review;
- high-risk or departing-user offboarding target;
- incident-reporting and response owner;
- vendor/data-flow inventory proportional to risk.

For any AI/camera/LMS pilot, require a documented privacy and AI impact review addressing purpose, data map, notice/consent where applicable, retention, access, security, accuracy testing, human review, correction path, and prohibition on unreviewed adverse employment/student decisions.

NIST provides a Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 quick-start guide specifically for small and medium organizations, including schools; it is an appropriate proportional reference model.

## R7 — Add government-contracting file integrity and conflict controls

### Finding

The source plan has an opportunity register and bid/no-bid logic. A government-facing organization also needs a controlled solicitation-to-closeout record and clear treatment of outside interests.

### Required revision

For every material government opportunity, record and review:

- authoritative solicitation and amendments;
- representations, registrations, exclusions, certifications, and renewal dates;
- bid/no-bid authority and conflict/organizational-conflict analysis;
- partner/subcontractor due diligence and written scope;
- pricing assumptions, direct/indirect costs, cash timing, and margin;
- proposal version and authorized submitter;
- contract deliverables, invoicing, acceptance, modification, renewal, termination, and closeout;
- separation of AK9I opportunity information from personal or outside-company use.

No informal relationship should determine abandonment or pursuit, and no opportunity should be pursued without performance capacity and economics.

## R8 — Replace subjective gates with evidence-based go/no-go decisions

### Finding

The source plan says growth can proceed when gates are “substantially” met. That leaves room for optimism or pressure to override a material red condition.

### Required revision

At Days 14, 30, 60, and 90, produce a signed or durably recorded gate package with:

- criterion;
- status: `GREEN / YELLOW / RED / UNKNOWN`;
- evidence source and date;
- accountable owner;
- open exception and compensating control;
- decision authority;
- explicit disposition: `PROCEED / PROCEED WITH LIMITS / HOLD / STOP`.

### Mandatory growth vetoes

Do not materially increase top-of-funnel spend or starts while any of the following is RED or UNKNOWN without an explicitly documented emergency exception:

- scheduled payroll funding/accuracy;
- current-student continuity;
- qualified instructor/dog/facility/equipment capacity;
- immediate safety or animal-welfare risk;
- program/approval/course-fact integrity;
- active pipeline ownership and response capability;
- critical account continuity;
- evidence to support the claims used in the campaign.

## R9 — Add data confidence, safety, control-effectiveness, and payroll KPIs

### Finding

The source scorecard is strong but needs a small set of guardrail metrics that make the turnaround safe and auditable.

### Required executive additions

- payroll paid accurately on the scheduled date;
- 13-week forecast weekly variance and explanation;
- next-two-payroll funding status;
- bank/payment reconciliations completed and independently reviewed;
- sampled spend-control pass rate and overdue exceptions;
- current-student continuity/capacity red conditions;
- canine/student/employee safety and animal-welfare incidents overdue for disposition;
- required training/evaluation records complete and current;
- critical systems with MFA, backup administration, and verified recovery;
- KPI/data-confidence rating: `verified / reconciled / provisional / unknown`;
- compliance/calendar obligations due or overdue;
- closed-loop journey records passing end-to-end trace testing.

No KPI should be used for compensation or discipline until its definition, controllability, data quality, and gaming risk are reviewed.

## R10 — Reconcile the plan with actual engagement capacity and calendar

### Finding

The Day 1–14 source is an excellent sequence, but it reads as fourteen consecutive workdays, including weekend analysis. The engagement and onsite schedule may not support that assumption.

### Required revision

- Treat Day numbers as elapsed transformation days unless a workday is explicitly scheduled.
- Publish a real resource-loaded calendar showing onsite days, remote analysis, CEO availability, department availability, travel, evidence owners, and who performs work between meetings.
- Do not assume unpaid or unapproved weekend work.
- Assign work to functional owners; the Transformation Lead should install and coordinate the system, not become the permanent owner of every process.
- Carry incomplete evidence forward transparently rather than compressing it into an artificial deadline.

# Final reviewer additions to the operating doctrine

The following rules are incorporated into the final executive edition:

1. **Payroll, safety, animal welfare, student continuity, and regulatory integrity are veto conditions—not merely KPIs.**
2. **A control is complete only when it has an owner, procedure, system/record, evidence, exception path, and test result.**
3. **A critical process is durable only when it has a primary owner and backup.**
4. **Growth requires capacity, claim integrity, attribution, and an explicit gate decision.**
5. **No constructive-discharge tactics, public accusations, rumor-based discipline, or unapproved compensation promises.**
6. **Strategic bets must identify the next falsifiable question, the cash/capacity consumed, and a stop condition.**
7. **Day 90 is a management-system handoff—not a declaration that transformation is finished.**

The source plan is **strategically approved**. The revisions above are not a rejection of its structure; they make that structure implementable under the actual risk profile described in the leadership source and the operating/regulatory context of a vocational working-dog organization.
