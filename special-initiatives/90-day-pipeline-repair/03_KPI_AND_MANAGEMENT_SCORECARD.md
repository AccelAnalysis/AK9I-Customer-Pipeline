# KPI & Management Scorecard — 90-Day Turnaround

## Purpose

This scorecard converts the turnaround into observable management. It is intentionally divided into:

- **Current measured value** — populated only from authoritative evidence.
- **Proposed control target** — a planning standard requiring executive adoption; it is not a claim about current performance.
- **Baseline-derived target** — not set until sufficient baseline data exists.

Where the current baseline is unknown, the correct initial value is `UNKNOWN — BASELINE REQUIRED`, not a guessed number.

## Scorecard design principles

1. Measure the full journey, not just top-of-funnel activity.
2. Give each KPI one accountable owner.
3. Pair lagging outcomes with leading indicators.
4. Separate volume, conversion, velocity, quality, capacity, and control.
5. Do not set arbitrary conversion goals before the denominator and baseline are trustworthy.
6. Make exceptions actionable: every RED/YELLOW metric produces an owner, next action, and due date.
7. Use the smallest useful KPI set. Additional diagnostics belong beneath the scorecard, not on the executive page.

# Management layers beneath the executive scorecard

## 1. Cash and financial control diagnostics

Track:

- cash on hand;
- next two payroll obligations;
- weekly expected receipts vs actual receipts;
- AR aging buckets;
- AP due within 7/14/30 days;
- debt/tax/critical regulatory obligations;
- tuition receipts;
- canine sales receipts/margin where determinable;
- contract/service receipts/margin where determinable;
- discretionary spend by category;
- unapproved/exception spend;
- recurring subscription/vendor spend;
- forecast variance and explanation.

**Decision question:** What threatens payroll, instruction, compliance, or continuity before the next review?

## 2. Admissions/pipeline diagnostics

Track counts and age by governed stage after stage validation:

- inquiry/lead;
- qualified prospect;
- applicant;
- enrollment-ready;
- enrolled;
- confirmed start;
- started;
- closed/lost/hold with reason.

Also track:

- original source/referral source where available;
- record owner;
- last meaningful contact;
- next action/due date;
- applications missing required items;
- funding-route delays;
- stale opportunities;
- lost reasons;
- reactivation candidates.

**Decision question:** Where are qualified people getting stuck, and why?

## 3. Student-success diagnostics

Track by program/cohort without placing student PII in GitHub:

- planned starts vs actual starts;
- active students;
- attendance-risk count;
- academic/performance-risk count;
- service/housing/transport risk where relevant;
- intervention count and closure;
- withdrawals and coded reasons;
- completions;
- completion timing;
- instructor/student capacity ratio or another approved capacity measure;
- open student complaints/service-recovery items.

**Decision question:** Which current students are most likely not to complete successfully without intervention?

## 4. Outcomes/placement diagnostics

Track clearly distinct evidence states:

- completed/graduate;
- outcome goal;
- placement assistance offered/provided;
- employer interaction;
- interview;
- offer;
- accepted offer;
- employment start;
- qualifying self-employment;
- continuing employment;
- verified outcome;
- seeking;
- not seeking/declined assistance where allowed by governing definitions;
- unreachable;
- insufficient evidence/unknown;
- last verification/follow-up date.

**Decision question:** What is verified, what remains unknown, and what action closes the gap?

## 5. Employer/partner diagnostics

Track:

- organization;
- relationship class;
- AK9I owner;
- current objective;
- current hiring/opportunity need;
- last meaningful activity;
- next action;
- candidates/recruiter interactions where authoritative systems permit;
- interviews/offers/verified outcomes attributable to relationship;
- referral demand attributable to relationship;
- feedback provided to AK9I.

**Decision question:** Which relationships are actually producing value, and what is the next mutual action?

## 6. Advocacy/reputation diagnostics

Track:

- advocacy-eligible population;
- feedback requested/completed;
- positive/neutral/negative feedback distribution if an approved instrument is used;
- service-recovery cases;
- review requested/completed;
- testimonial requested/completed/approved for use;
- referral asked/received;
- resulting referral stage/outcome;
- consent/provenance status.

**Decision question:** Are successful experiences turning into defensible reputation and qualified demand without ignoring dissatisfied customers?

## 7. Marketing diagnostics

Track by source/campaign:

- spend;
- responses/leads;
- qualified entrants;
- applications;
- confirmed starts;
- starts;
- downstream completion/outcome when enough time has passed;
- CPL as a diagnostic only;
- cost per qualified entrant;
- cost per start;
- eventual contribution/margin where defensible.

**Decision question:** Which channels create customers AK9I can successfully serve—not merely attention?

# KPI ownership card template

Every KPI owner should receive a compact definition:

- **KPI name**
- **Why it matters**
- **Definition/numerator/denominator**
- **Authoritative source**
- **Current baseline**
- **Target state** (`PROPOSED`, `APPROVED`, or `BASELINE-DERIVED`)
- **Reporting cadence**
- **Thresholds / exception logic**
- **Owner**
- **Contributors**
- **Actions the owner may take without further approval**
- **Escalation trigger**

No KPI should be used for compensation or discipline until its definition, controllability, data quality, and unintended-incentive risk have been reviewed.

# Proposed first-30-day control standards

The following are proposed operating controls, not existing AK9I performance claims:

1. 100% of active students have a visible current status, risk state, owner, and next action.
2. 100% of material in-scope discretionary purchases follow the approved workflow.
3. 100% of critical organizational accounts have adequate continuity administration where technically possible.
4. 100% of active pipeline records in the implemented operating scope have an owner, next action, and due date.
5. 100% of outcome-eligible graduates entering the new process have a follow-up owner and evidence state.
6. 0 known unresolved canonical conflicts in course duration/price/approval claims are deliberately amplified in new marketing.
7. 100% of key roles have a defined purpose, recurring responsibilities, and KPI scorecard by Day 30.

Conversion, withdrawal, completion, placement, acquisition-cost, and revenue targets should be set only after the baseline is validated.
