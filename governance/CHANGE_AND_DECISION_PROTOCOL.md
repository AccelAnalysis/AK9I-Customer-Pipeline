# Change and Decision Protocol

## Decision classes

### Local lane decision
Within charter and approved work-packet boundary; no shared semantic change. The lane may decide independently and records material decisions.

### Shared contract decision
Changes shared pipeline behavior/meaning. Requires shared-contract authority coordinated by lane 01 with affected lanes; program-architecture implications go to lane 00.

### Program architecture decision
Changes mission, lanes, sequencing doctrine, governance precedence, or other program architecture. Requires Control Room authority subject to executive direction.

### Business/executive decision
Requires authorized AK9I human authority. Lanes must escalate rather than manufacture approval.

Material decisions are recorded in `control-room/DECISION_LOG.md` with date, decision, class, authority, affected requirements/work packets, rationale/evidence, and consequences.
