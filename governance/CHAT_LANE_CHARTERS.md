# Chat Lane Charters

These ten lanes are permanent unless program architecture is explicitly changed. Functional specialties normally remain bounded work packets rather than new permanent lanes.

## 00 — AK9I Pipeline Control Room

**Mission:** Coordinate the full AK9I Customer Pipeline program so independently executed work produces one improving customer journey.

**Owns:** program mission; priorities; sequencing; requirements; tracker; dependency map; bottleneck identification; work-packet allocation; cross-lane conflict resolution; current program state; risk prioritization; wave management; executive escalations; health-scorecard interpretation.

**Does not normally own:** functional implementation; campaigns; admissions operations; placement implementation; instructional implementation; independent acceptance.

**Fundamental question:** What is currently preventing more qualified prospects from becoming successful AK9I customers/graduates and advocates, and what work can safely progress in parallel to improve that constraint?

## 01 — Shared Pipeline Platform & Intelligence

**Mission:** Make the customer pipeline observable, consistent, measurable, and reusable across all functional lanes.

**Owns:** CRM/pipeline architecture; shared lifecycle; canonical concepts; stage definitions; source attribution; shared data definitions; canonical IDs; next-action conventions; measurement/dashboard/analytics definitions; cross-lane data/interface contracts; common communications state where applicable.

**Does not own:** marketing, admissions, placement, or instructional outcomes.

**Fundamental question:** Can every lane reliably understand the same customer, lifecycle state, and measurement definitions?

## 02 — Advocacy & Reputation

**Mission:** Turn successful AK9I experiences into measurable reputation, feedback, referrals, testimonials, and repeatable advocacy.

**Owns:** feedback; approved satisfaction measurement/NPS if adopted; reviews; testimonials; success stories; referral asks; advocacy workflows; advocate qualification; reputation feedback loops.

**Fundamental question:** Are successful AK9I experiences systematically becoming evidence, reputation, referrals, and better future demand?

## 03 — Outcomes, Placement & Alumni

**Mission:** Make post-completion outcomes visible, supported, measurable, and connected to employers and alumni.

**Owns:** graduation-to-outcome process; outcome goals; career support; employer matching/interactions; interviews; offers; verified outcomes; graduate follow-up; alumni; employer feedback; outcome evidence.

**Distinction:** Owns individual graduate outcomes. Lane 07 owns the broader reusable external partnership/channel network.

**Fundamental question:** What happens after completion, and can AK9I demonstrate and improve the resulting outcome?

## 04 — Learning & Student Success

**Mission:** Ensure an enrolled/started customer receives the learning, support, progression, and completion experience the program intends.

**Owns:** training onboarding; student experience; learning/process support; progress visibility; evaluations; retention; completion; learning-related feedback; barriers to completion; student-success improvement.

**Fundamental question:** Are students who begin the program supported through the intended learning experience and successful completion?

## 05 — Enrollment & Admissions

**Mission:** Reliably convert qualified interest into appropriately enrolled customers ready to begin.

**Owns:** Inquiry → qualification → application → enrollment readiness → enrollment → confirmed start; admissions workflow; response/follow-up; application friction; documentation process; funding/payment pathway coordination where relevant; enrollment communications; abandonment recovery; conversion measurement; start readiness.

**Fundamental question:** Can every appropriate qualified prospect move from interest to a legitimate, confirmed start without preventable friction or lost ownership?

## 06 — Demand Generation & Marketing

**Mission:** Generate qualified, attributable demand that can successfully enter the AK9I pipeline.

**Owns:** positioning; segmentation; website conversion; organic social; LinkedIn; campaigns; content; SEO; landing pages; lead generation; authorized paid acquisition; campaign attribution implementation; remarketing; creative; marketing performance.

**Does not own:** lifecycle definitions; admissions qualification standards; unsupported placement/outcome claims.

**Fundamental question:** Are marketing efforts producing qualified, attributable pipeline entrants rather than merely attention?

## 07 — Strategic Partnerships & Channels

**Mission:** Build external relationships that repeatedly generate qualified candidates, employers, outcomes, referrals, credibility, or other durable pipeline value.

**Owns as applicable:** employers; agencies; law-enforcement relationships; military/veteran channels; workforce channels; recruiters; referral organizations; industry/institutional partners; strategic channel development; partner activation; partner-produced pipeline.

**Fundamental question:** Which external relationships can repeatedly improve demand, enrollment, outcomes, or credibility, and are those relationships producing measurable pipeline movement?

## 08 — Independent Acceptance

**Mission:** Independently determine whether claimed pipeline improvements satisfy approved requirements.

**Independence:** Does not implement functional production work merely to make a candidate pass.

**Method:** original requirement → authority → required observable outcome → exact candidate → evidence → disposition.

**Owns:** acceptance plans; evidence review; requirement verification; dispositions; residual-risk findings; assurance backlog.

**Fundamental question:** What was actually required, and does objective evidence show the candidate satisfies it?

## 09 — Pipeline Integration / Journey QA

**Mission:** Verify that individually functioning/accepted components behave as one coherent AK9I customer journey.

**Owns:** cross-lane journey tests; handoff tests; shared-state continuity; integration defects; end-to-end scenarios; organizational seam analysis; critical-journey regression.

**Does not replace:** Independent Acceptance.

**Fundamental question:** Can an actual customer move successfully through the intended journey without falling through gaps between functions?
