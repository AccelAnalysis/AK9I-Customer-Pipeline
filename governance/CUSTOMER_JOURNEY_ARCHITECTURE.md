# Customer Journey Architecture

## Canonical initial journey

```text
UNKNOWN AUDIENCE
  ↓
AWARE
  ↓
ENGAGED
  ↓
INQUIRY / LEAD
  ↓
QUALIFIED PROSPECT
  ↓
APPLICANT
  ↓
ENROLLMENT-READY
  ↓
ENROLLED
  ↓
CONFIRMED START
  ↓
STARTED
  ↓
ACTIVE STUDENT
  ↓
PROGRAM COMPLETION
  ↓
GRADUATE
  ↓
POST-GRADUATION OUTCOME
  ↓
ALUMNI
  ↓
ADVOCATE / REFERRAL SOURCE
  └────────────────────────→ NEW QUALIFIED DEMAND
```

This is initial governing architecture, not a claim that AK9I currently records each state or that detailed transition criteria are already known.

## Common-path doctrine

If validated pathways differ by program, audience, funding route, referral route, employer sponsorship, military/veteran status, agency route, or other legitimate dimension, model them as governed paths through this common journey rather than creating incompatible lifecycle systems.

## Closed loop

`MARKET → DEMAND → ENROLLMENT → LEARNING / DELIVERY → COMPLETION → OUTCOME → ADVOCACY → REVIEWS + REFERRALS + REPUTATION + PARTNERSHIPS → BETTER QUALIFIED DEMAND → MARKET`

## End-to-beginning improvement sequence

Advocacy/Reputation ↑ Outcomes/Placement/Alumni ↑ Learning/Student Success ↑ Enrollment/Admissions ↑ Demand Generation. Strategic Partnerships and Shared Platform operate horizontally.

The structural principle is: do not aggressively increase top-of-funnel volume until downstream reception, support, observability, and intended outcomes are sufficiently understood. Useful marketing may proceed safely in parallel.
