# MKT-001 — Inventory Current Demand Sources and Marketing Measurement

- **Objective:** Inventory actual demand channels, conversion paths, attribution, and measurement.
- **Original requirements:** MKT-001, ATTR-001, PRIV-001
- **Business reason:** Marketing should generate qualified attributable pipeline entrants, not just attention.
- **Current state:** UNKNOWN — BASELINE REQUIRED.
- **Owner lane:** 06
- **Human accountable owner:** UNKNOWN — BASELINE REQUIRED
- **Inputs:** channel accounts/reports, website/analytics, admissions feedback.
- **Dependencies:** none for discovery; attribution convergence YELLOW on SHR-003.
- **Dependency classification:** GREEN for inventory
- **Shared contracts consumed:** source attribution, lifecycle, approved outcome/advocacy evidence.
- **Authorized actions:** inventory channels/campaigns/content/website paths and qualified-demand measurement.
- **Expected output:** current demand map, measurement gaps, PCRs where shared contracts are missing.
- **KPI / observable outcome affected:** inquiries, qualified inquiries, source mix, contribution, acquisition economics when authoritative.
- **Evidence required:** channel/report evidence without PII.
- **Acceptance criteria:** vanity metrics separated from pipeline outcomes; unsupported outcome promises identified, not repeated.
- **Integration requirement:** 06→01→05 and 02/03 evidence loop.
- **Explicit non-ownership:** admissions qualification standards or unsupported placement claims.
- **Stop boundary:** do not invent channel performance or budgets.
- **Status:** READY
- **Next action:** inspect actual channels and measurement.
