# LRN-001 Student Journey Discovery Framework

## Purpose

Establish the evidence-backed current state of AK9I's student journey from accepted start through learning/support, completion, and handoff to Outcomes. This artifact is a discovery framework, not a claim about current AK9I operations.

## Governing boundaries

- Keep student/customer PII out of GitHub.
- Use aggregate, de-identified, synthetic, or authoritative-system references only.
- Distinguish observed actual process from intended/policy process.
- Do not redefine admissions/enrollment criteria owned by Lane 05.
- Do not invent STARTED, ACTIVE STUDENT, withdrawal/non-completion, PROGRAM COMPLETION, or GRADUATE criteria. Shared lifecycle semantics are governed through Lane 01 / SHR-002.
- Do not publish instructional, completion, retention, satisfaction, or placement claims without authoritative evidence.

## Discovery questions

### 1. Entry from Enrollment to Learning

Determine from actual evidence:

- What event makes an enrolled person a confirmed start?
- What event proves the person actually started training?
- Which system/process records the start, timestamp, program/cohort, and accountable owner?
- What information is handed from Admissions to the learning team?
- What happens when a confirmed student does not start?
- Who owns recovery/no-show follow-up and where is that disposition recorded?

### 2. Training onboarding

Observe:

- first-day/first-week onboarding steps;
- orientation, expectations, schedule, materials/equipment, access, and required acknowledgements;
- responsible staff role(s);
- evidence that onboarding occurred;
- common onboarding failures and recovery path;
- differences by program/pathway/cohort if validated.

### 3. Active learning and progression

Observe:

- curriculum/progression checkpoints actually used;
- attendance or participation signals, if applicable;
- practical/skills checkpoints;
- evaluations/assessments and scoring/disposition logic;
- who reviews progress and at what cadence;
- where progress evidence is stored;
- how a student at risk is identified;
- whether the next support action has a clear owner and due date;
- whether unresolved barriers can age without ownership.

### 4. Student support and barriers

Inventory the actual mechanisms for:

- academic/instructional support;
- attendance/scheduling issues;
- equipment/material issues;
- financial/administrative barriers that affect continued participation without redefining Admissions policy;
- transportation/logistics where relevant;
- behavioral/conduct/safety processes where relevant and authorized;
- accessibility/accommodation routing where applicable;
- complaint/escalation path;
- re-entry, make-up, remediation, pause/leave, withdrawal, or dismissal pathways where they actually exist.

For each support mechanism identify trigger, owner, system, next action, escalation, closure evidence, and known failure mode.

### 5. Retention, withdrawal, and non-completion

Establish what AK9I actually records for students who do not proceed normally:

- withdrawal/non-completion categories;
- voluntary vs administrative distinctions where they exist;
- last-attendance/last-participation evidence where applicable;
- reason capture and confidence;
- recovery/re-entry eligibility or process;
- accountable owner;
- reporting source and known data-quality limits.

Do not create new lifecycle semantics locally; raise a PCR when shared definitions are required.

### 6. Completion

Determine the authoritative operational completion event:

- required curriculum/skills/evaluation conditions;
- administrative requirements, if part of the accepted definition;
- who determines completion;
- source system/report/record;
- completion date definition;
- correction/reversal process;
- distinction, if any, between program completion and graduate status.

Until validated through the shared lifecycle process, repository statements must remain descriptive observations rather than canonical stage definitions.

### 7. Completion handoff to Outcomes

Trace the actual 04→03 handoff:

- trigger;
- required shared state;
- information transferred;
- responsible sender and receiver;
- timing/SLA if one actually exists;
- source and destination systems/processes;
- confirmation that Outcomes received responsibility;
- failure/recovery path;
- how a completed student avoids becoming unowned after graduation.

## Minimum evidence request

Use authoritative or de-identified sources. Useful evidence includes:

1. Current written or system-configured student onboarding process, if any.
2. De-identified screenshots/field lists or reports showing start, active, attendance/progress, evaluation, withdrawal/non-completion, and completion information.
3. Aggregate student counts by relevant state/time period only when definitions and source are known.
4. Current evaluation/assessment forms or rubrics, with no student-identifying data.
5. Current support/intervention workflow or examples scrubbed of PII.
6. Current completion/graduation checklist/process and authoritative record source.
7. Evidence of the current 05→04 and 04→03 handoffs.
8. Role/accountability information for training onboarding, progress review, intervention, completion determination, and Outcomes handoff.
9. Any existing student feedback/satisfaction mechanism and aggregate reporting.
10. Known recurring barriers, supported by de-identified operational evidence rather than anecdote alone where possible.

## Barrier classification

Classify only evidence-backed barriers:

- Handoff/ownership
- Information/data visibility
- Scheduling/capacity
- Instructional/progression
- Assessment/evaluation
- Student support
- Materials/equipment
- Administrative/process
- Communication
- System/tooling
- Policy/definition
- External/logistical
- Other validated category

For each barrier record observable symptom, affected journey step, evidence, frequency/scale if known, current owner, current recovery method, and whether a cross-lane dependency/PCR is required.

## Cross-lane seams to verify

### Lane 05 → Lane 04

Required question: can a legitimately confirmed starter enter training with ownership and required state intact?

### Lane 04 → Lane 03

Required question: can an accepted program completion become an owned post-graduation outcome case without manual ambiguity or loss?

### Lane 01 shared lifecycle

If discovery shows AK9I-specific definitions needed for STARTED, ACTIVE STUDENT, withdrawal/non-completion, or PROGRAM COMPLETION, record the observed business facts and request canonicalization through SHR-002/PCR rather than defining a Lane 04-only lifecycle.

## Completion conditions for LRN-001 discovery

LRN-001 may be claimed `IMPLEMENTATION COMPLETE` for discovery only when:

- the actual current journey is mapped from start through completion;
- intended process is separately identified where known;
- authoritative systems/sources and accountable owners are identified or explicitly documented as gaps;
- completion and 04→03 handoff are observable, or the precise gap is documented;
- evidence-backed barriers/unknowns are recorded;
- any material cross-lane dependencies/PCRs are durable;
- no PII is stored in GitHub;
- tracker/current-state artifacts reflect the resulting status.

Lane 08, not Lane 04, determines acceptance of the requirement candidate. Lane 09 separately verifies 05→04→03 continuity.
